# Improve Share Certificate & Investment Tracking

The current share certificate only displays the **Face Value** of the shares. While this is legally correct, TapasHub is an AI-powered business and investment platform, so shareholders should also be able to see how much they actually invested and how their investment has grown over time.

## Do NOT change the accounting logic

Keep the existing accounting structure:

* **Share Capital = Number of Shares × Face Value**
* **Share Premium = Investment Amount − Share Capital**
* Store both separately in the database.

Example:

* Shares: 769
* Face Value: ₹100
* Share Capital: ₹76,900
* Investment Amount: ₹1,25,000
* Share Premium: ₹48,100

This follows standard corporate accounting practices.

# Company Valuation Engine

Automatically calculate:

### Share Capital

Share Capital = Outstanding Shares × Face Value

### Share Premium

Share Premium = Total Investment Received − Share Capital

### Net Worth

Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities

### Book Value Per Share

Book Value Per Share = Net Worth ÷ Outstanding Shares

### Estimated Market Value
