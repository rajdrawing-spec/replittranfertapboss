## Replit AI Prompt – Redesign the Finance & Fund Allocation System

The current **Fund Allocation** module is confusing and needs to be redesigned to reflect our actual company structure.

### Company Structure

**TapasHub** is the parent company.

All investors invest only in **TapasHub**.

Shareholder investments, equity, and funding are collected into the **TapasHub Main Account**.

The subsidiaries do **not** receive investments directly from shareholders.

Instead, TapasHub allocates funds to each subsidiary based on approved budgets and business requirements.

Example:

* TapasHub raises ₹50,00,000 from shareholders.
* The money stays in the TapasHub treasury.
* TapasHub approves budgets for subsidiaries.
* Example allocation:

  * HugFab – ₹5,00,000
  * TikkaTails – ₹5,00,000
  * TottoToy – ₹5,00,000
  * TargetGum – ₹5,00,000
* Additional funds can be allocated later whenever required.

---

### Finance Module Structure

Create a clear finance system with the following sections:

### 1. TapasHub Treasury

This is the central finance dashboard.

Show:

* Total Shareholder Investment
* Available Cash Balance
* Total Funds Allocated
* Remaining Balance
* Investment History
* Shareholder Equity
* Capital Raised
* Company Valuation
* Cash Flow Summary

---

### 2. Fund Allocation

Create a dedicated **Fund Allocation** page.

Allow administrators to:

* Select Subsidiary
* Enter Allocation Amount
* Allocation Date
* Purpose
* Budget Category
* Notes
* Approval Status

Each allocation should create a transaction record.

Example:

TapasHub → TikkaTails → ₹5,00,000

Status: Approved

---

### 3. Subsidiary Finance Dashboard

Each subsidiary should have its own finance page showing:

* Funds Received from TapasHub
* Current Available Balance
* Total Expenses
* Remaining Budget
* Monthly Spending
* Expense Categories
* Budget Utilization %
* Financial Reports

Important:

Subsidiaries **cannot** issue shares or collect investments directly.

Their funding source is only TapasHub.

---

### 4. Expense Management

Each subsidiary can record expenses.

Categories:

* Salaries
* Marketing
* Technology
* Office
* Software
* Operations
* Manufacturing
* Shipping
* Legal
* Miscellaneous

Each expense should reduce the subsidiary's available balance.

---

### 5. Financial Reports

Generate reports for:

TapasHub

* Total Investments
* Total Allocations
* Treasury Balance
* Cash Flow
* Profit & Loss
* Budget vs Actual

Subsidiaries

* Funds Received
* Total Expenses
* Remaining Budget
* Monthly Reports
* Expense Breakdown

---

### 6. Transaction History

Maintain a complete audit trail.

Every financial transaction should include:

* Transaction ID
* Date
* Source
* Destination
* Amount
* Category
* Description
* Created By
* Approval Status

Nothing should be deletable—only reversible with proper approval.

---

### 7. Approval Workflow

Require approval for:

* Fund Allocation
* Large Expenses
* Budget Changes
* Additional Funding Requests

Track who approved and when.

---

### 8. Dashboard Visualization

Create easy-to-understand charts and cards showing:

* Total Treasury
* Allocated Funds
* Available Funds
* Subsidiary Budgets
* Monthly Expenses
* Budget Utilization
* Cash Flow
* Investment Distribution

The dashboard should be simple enough that investors, shareholders, and team members can immediately understand how money flows from **TapasHub → Subsidiaries → Expenses**.

---

### 9. Data Flow

Implement this financial flow:

**Shareholders → TapasHub Treasury → Fund Allocation → Subsidiaries → Expenses**

This should be the only funding workflow.

---

### Final Goal

Redesign the finance module so it follows standard corporate accounting practices with a clear parent-company treasury model. The interface should be intuitive, transparent, and easy for shareholders, administrators, and subsidiary managers to understand, with complete financial visibility and audit history.
