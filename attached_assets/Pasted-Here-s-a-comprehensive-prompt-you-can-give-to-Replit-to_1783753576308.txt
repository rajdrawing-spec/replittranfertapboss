# Audit Trail

Every equity transaction must be logged.

Record

* User
* Date
* Time
* IP
* Previous Value
* New Value
* Approval Status

No share transaction should ever be deleted.

Only allow reversal with a complete audit trail.

---

# Validation Rules

Prevent inconsistent data.

For example:

* `Outstanding Shares × Face Value` should equal the recorded share capital.
* Ownership percentages across all shareholders must total **100%** (unless treasury shares are excluded by design).
* Prevent issuing more shares than the authorized share capital.
* Automatically recalculate all ownership percentages after issuing, transferring, buying back, or cancelling shares.
* Keep parent and subsidiary valuations separate while also maintaining a consolidated group view.
* Prevent duplicate certificate numbers and duplicate share issuance.
