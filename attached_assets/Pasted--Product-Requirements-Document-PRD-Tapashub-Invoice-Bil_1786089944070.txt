# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Tapashub Invoice & Billing Management System

**Version:** 1.0
**Product:** Tapashub ERP
**Module:** Invoice, Billing & Financial Documents
**Platform:** Web + Mobile
**Multi-Tenant:** Yes

---

# 1. Objective

Create a professional **Invoice & Billing module** inside Tapashub where every company under Tapashub can generate invoices, quotations, purchase orders, proforma invoices, credit notes, debit notes, receipts, and downloadable PDFs from one centralized platform.

The system must support **multiple companies**, **multiple brands**, **GST**, **international invoices**, **digital signatures**, and **professional PDF generation**.

---

# 2. Companies Using This Module

Every company should have its own branding, invoice numbering, GST details, logo, and templates.

Examples

* Tapashub Pvt Ltd

* Pepalworks

## Expected Outcome

This module will provide a **centralized invoicing and financial document system** for every company under Tapashub while maintaining strict **company-level data isolation**. Each brand will have its own identity, numbering, tax settings, and templates, while finance teams can efficiently create, manage, download, email, and report on invoices from a single platform. It will integrate seamlessly with Inventory, CRM, Accounting, HR, and Analytics, making Tapashub a complete multi-company ERP solution.
