## Design Inspiration

I would redesign TapBoss closer to these products:

* **Linear** → clean typography and spacing
* **Slack** → best chat experience
* **Notion** → simple layouts and readability
* **Arc Browser** → premium animations
* **Stripe Dashboard** → excellent data presentation
* **Apple** → refined visual polish

The biggest change I would make is to move away from a "traditional ERP dashboard" look toward a **modern productivity workspace**. That means larger typography, more whitespace, clearer visual hierarchy, richer cards, subtle gradients, and thoughtful micro-interactions so the interface feels alive instead of flat.
