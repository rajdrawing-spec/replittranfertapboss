**Critical Concern: Shareholder Access & Share Calculation Logic**

I've identified a major security and data architecture issue in the Shareholder module.

### 1. Company Isolation (Multi-Tenant Security)

Currently, if I create a **Shareholder** from the **Super Admin**, that shareholder appears to have access to **all companies**.

This should **never** happen.

A shareholder belongs to **only one company (subdomain)**, so how can they view data from every company?

The system must enforce strict tenant isolation.

**Expected behavior:**

* Every shareholder must be linked to a single `company_id`.
* After login, they should only access their assigned company's workspace.
* They must never see:

  * Other companies
  * Other companies' shareholders
  * Other companies' valuation
  * Other companies' reports
  * Other companies' financial data

Only the **Super Admin** should have permission to view data across all companies.

---

### 2. Incorrect Shareholder Count

Looking at the Analytics page, it shows:

**Shareholders: 37 across portfolio**

This logic also appears incorrect.

One person may own shares in **multiple companies**.

For example:

* Raj Kumar owns shares in Company A
* Raj Kumar also owns shares in Company B
* Raj Kumar also owns shares in Company C

This is **one shareholder**, not three different shareholders.

The system must distinguish between:

* **Unique Shareholders** (unique people)
* **Shareholder Records** (ownership entries per company)

Example:

| Person    | Company   |
| --------- | --------- |
| Raj Kumar | Company A |
| Raj Kumar | Company B |
| Raj Kumar | Company C |
| John      | Company A |

Results should be:

* Unique Shareholders = **2**
* Shareholding Records = **4**

If the dashboard shows **37**, please clarify whether this is:

* 37 unique people, or
* 37 shareholder records across all companies.
