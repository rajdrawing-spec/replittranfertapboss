For **Tapas Hub**, the UI should feel like a premium collaboration platform (Slack + Microsoft Teams + Discord + WhatsApp Business), while keeping your **black, white, and blue** brand identity.

# Tapas Hub Design Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity

**Primary Colors**

* ⚫ Black: `#111111`
* ⚪ White: `#FFFFFF`
* 🔵 Primary Blue: `#2DA8FF`
* 🔹 Light Blue: `#EAF6FF`
* 🟢 Online: `#22C55E`
* 🔴 Urgent: `#EF4444`
* 🟡 Warning: `#FBBF24`

Use blue only for actions and highlights.

# 21. Premium Details

* Use your **Tapas Hub logo** as the default avatar for company groups.
* Add a subtle **blue gradient** in headers and sidebars to match the logo.
* Use **glassmorphism** only for popups and dialogs, not the main chat area.
* Maintain **8px spacing** between related elements for a clean layout.
* Ensure all components are **responsive** for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Follow **WCAG AA** accessibility standards with proper contrast, keyboard navigation, and visible focus states.

This design direction will give Tapas Hub a polished, enterprise-grade appearance while staying consistent with your brand colors and making the platform feel modern and professional.
