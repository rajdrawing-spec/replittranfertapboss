# AI Business Intelligence Module - Additional Requirements

In addition to the AI Business Intelligence & Growth Module already planned, please implement the following advanced features to make TapasHub a true AI-powered Business Operating System.

---

# 17. AI Model Integration (Free AI APIs)

Integrate one or more **free or low-cost AI models** that can analyze all company data and provide intelligent business insights.

The AI architecture should be modular so different models can be configured later.

Suggested providers:

* Google Gemini API (Free Tier)
* OpenRouter (Free Models)
* Groq API (Fast Free Models)
* DeepSeek API
* Qwen Models
* Local LLM support using Ollama (optional for self-hosting)

The system should allow switching between AI providers without changing the application logic.

---

# 18. AI Business Analysis Engine

The AI should analyze every company using all available business data, including:

* Finance
* Treasury
* Working Capital
* Fund Allocation
* Sales
* Marketing
* CRM
* Inventory
* HR
* Payroll
* Projects
* Customer Feedback
* Internal Chat
* Company Profile
* Market Intelligence

The AI should automatically identify:

* Business strengths
* Weaknesses
* Opportunities
* Threats (SWOT Analysis)
* Revenue leaks
* Cost optimization opportunities
* Cash flow risks
* Growth opportunities
* Investment priorities
* Operational inefficiencies

---

# 19. Company Valuation & Shareholder Intelligence

Add a **Shareholder Dashboard** for every company.

Display:

* Estimated Company Valuation
* Business Growth Score
* Company Worth Over Time
* Estimated Enterprise Value
* Shareholder Equity
* Net Asset Value
* Revenue Growth Rate
* Profit Growth Rate
* Cash Growth
* Business Health Trend

If shareholder information exists, display:

* Shareholder Name
* Ownership Percentage
* Share Value
* Estimated Share Value Growth
* Return on Investment (ROI)
* Dividend History (if applicable)

The AI should estimate future share value based on business performance and explain the factors affecting the prediction.

**Note:** For private companies, this should be clearly labeled as an **AI estimate** based on company financials and assumptions, not an official market valuation.

---

# 20. AI Growth Prediction

Use historical company data to predict:

* Company growth over the next 3, 6, and 12 months
* Revenue growth
* Profit growth
* Business valuation growth
* Market share growth
* Customer growth
* Employee growth
* Cash flow trends

Each prediction should include:

* Confidence Score
* Risk Level
* Supporting Factors
* AI Explanation
* Recommended Actions

---

# 21. Market Demand & Competitor Analysis

Using the Company Profile and Market Intelligence, the AI should analyze:

* Industry demand
* Product demand
* Customer demand
* Geographic demand
* Seasonal demand
* Competitor pricing
* Competitor strengths
* Competitor weaknesses
* Market trends
* Emerging business opportunities

The AI should recommend:

* New products to launch
* Markets to enter
* Products to discontinue
* Pricing improvements
* Marketing improvements
* Competitive positioning strategies

---

# 22. Executive AI Decision Engine

The AI should function like a virtual executive team (CEO, CFO, COO, and CMO) and proactively answer questions such as:

* Should we invest more in this company?
* Should we reduce expenses?
* Which company deserves additional funding?
* Which products should be expanded?
* Which departments need improvement?
* Where should marketing budgets be increased or reduced?
* Which company is most profitable?
* Which company has the highest long-term growth potential?

Every recommendation must include:

* Business reasoning
* Supporting data
* Expected financial impact
* Estimated implementation effort
* Confidence Score
* Priority Level

---

# 23. AI Executive Reports

Automatically generate:

* Daily Business Summary
* Weekly Executive Report
* Monthly Business Review
* Quarterly Growth Analysis
* Annual Performance Report

Each report should include:

* KPIs
* Charts
* Trends
* Risks
* Opportunities
* AI Recommendations
* Action Plan

---

# 24. Future-Proof AI Architecture

Design the AI system to be modular and scalable.

Requirements:

* Support multiple AI providers
* AI service abstraction layer
* Configurable prompts
* Role-based AI permissions
* Background AI analysis jobs
* Real-time AI insights
* Explainable AI recommendations
* AI feedback loop to improve recommendation quality over time

---

# Final Goal

TapasHub should become an **AI-first Business Operating System** capable of understanding every company's business model, financial health, operations, market position, and long-term strategy.

The AI should act as a trusted business advisor that continuously analyzes all available data, predicts future performance, estimates business value, provides shareholder insights, detects risks, identifies growth opportunities, and delivers personalized recommendations to help every company make smarter decisions and grow sustainably.

**Important:** Any company valuation, share value growth, or shareholder return predictions for private companies must be clearly identified as AI-generated estimates based on internal business data and assumptions, not official financial or investment advice.
